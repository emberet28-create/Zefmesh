About Zefmesh

Zefmesh Furniture is a furniture manufacturer in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. It makes office, home and hotel furniture in its own workshop and sells through showrooms at Grand Mall, Megenagna and at Jakros.

The facts

What we make and where

What we make
Office seating, desks and conference tables; sofas, beds, dining and storage; contract furniture for hotels and restaurants.
Where it is made
Most pieces, from kitchens and beds to sofas and desks, are made in our own workshop in Kality, Addis Ababa.
What we import
Pieces that need materials or mechanisms we cannot source in Ethiopia, such as some mesh office chairs and lighting, come from partner manufacturers abroad. The same quality checks apply.
Import on request
For business projects we can import specific products or materials that a client asks for.
Where to see it
Grand Mall, Megenagna and the EBM building at Jakros.
Where we deliver
Across Addis Ababa, and to regional cities by arrangement, with assembly by our own team.
Who we work with
Families furnishing a home, and offices, banks, hotels, schools and government buyers.
Company
Zefmesh PLC, Addis Ababa. VAT invoices on every order.
